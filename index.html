Google Photos shared album

In Google Photos open an album → Share → Create link, then paste the link here. Photos load from Google as you play, and you can send someone a link that opens the game with the same album.

Advanced

The album page is read through a relay, because Google blocks browsers from reading it directly. Public relays are used by default; for reliability you can host your own (see worker/relay.js). Use {url} where the address goes.
